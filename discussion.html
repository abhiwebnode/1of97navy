Where the 1/97 Batch Talks

Our batch never stopped talking — we just moved from the messdecks to our phones. The 1/97 Batch runs an active community of discussion groups on WhatsApp, where shipmates connect every single day.

Our Discussion Lives on WhatsApp

The 1/97 Batch maintains multiple active WhatsApp groups covering every topic that matters to our community.

Batch Memories
Reliving the days at INS Chilka, ships we served on, shipmates we remember — stories that never get old.
Welfare & Support
Helping batchmates and their families navigate veterans' benefits, medical support, employment and more.
Reunion Planning
Coordinating the next time we get together — venue, dates, logistics and the excitement of seeing old faces.
Ships & Units
Conversations by ship — those who served together on the same vessel, reliving those specific memories.
General & Everything Else
News, achievements, humour, life updates — the full breadth of what makes this batch a family.

How to join our WhatsApp groups
Our groups are private and verified — open only to confirmed 1/97 Batch members. To be added, reach out to any existing group member or contact the batch admin directly. You will be verified and added to the relevant group based on your service details.

No public links are shared to protect our members' privacy.
