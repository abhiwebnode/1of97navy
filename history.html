THE BEGINNING

One Beginning. One Journey. One Brotherhood.

We are the men who joined the Indian Navy in January 1997. Our journey began together at INS Chilka, where we underwent six months of basic training before moving on to ship training and professional training.

From those early days of training, our paths took us to ships and establishments across the Indian Navy. We served in different places, performed different duties and experienced different chapters of naval life — but the foundation remained the same: the training, discipline, values and friendships forged together at the beginning of our careers.

OUR YEARS OF SERVICE

Different Ships. Different Establishments. One Batch.

Following our training, members of the 1/97 Batch went on to join ships and establishments across the Indian Navy.

Our individual careers took us in different directions. We served in different environments, took on different responsibilities and built our own professional experiences. Yet, wherever our service took us, we remained connected by the common experience of having started this journey together.

This section of our history will continue to grow as members contribute their service stories, ship details, photographs and memories.
