Who We Are

The 1/97 Batch — A Brotherhood Forged at Sea

We are the men who joined the Indian Navy in January 1997. Our journey began together at INS Chilka, with six months of basic training followed by ship and professional training. From there, we went on to serve aboard the Navy’s finest ships and at establishments across the country. The years may have taken us on different paths, but the values we learned, the memories we created, and the bonds we forged in service remain with us—in uniform and beyond.

This website is our shared home. A place to reconnect with shipmates, honour those no longer with us, share the stories of our service, and celebrate what we continue to achieve.
