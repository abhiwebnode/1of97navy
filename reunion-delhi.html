About This Reunion

Back in the Capital

The 5th reunion of the 1/97 batch brought us together in New Delhi — the heart of the nation. Old stories were retold, new ones were made, and the bond that began at INS Chilka proved it needs no uniform to stay strong.

Moments to Remember

Get-Together Dinner

153 shipmates gathered on the evening of 04 November 2025 for a dinner filled with old stories, hearty laughter, and the kind of camaraderie that only shipmates understand. The night ran long — and no one minded.
