About This Reunion

Back in the Capital

The 5th reunion of the 1/97 batch brought us together in New Delhi — the heart of the nation. Old stories were retold, new ones were made, and the bond that began at INS Chilka proved it needs no uniform to stay strong. The capital gave us the backdrop; the batch gave us everything else.
