About This Reunion

Back in the Capital

The 5th reunion of the 1/97 batch brought us together in New Delhi — the heart of the nation. Old stories were retold, new ones were made, and the bond that began at INS Chilka proved it needs no uniform to stay strong.

Full details and memories to be added. If you were there and would like to contribute, please reach out to the batch coordinator.
